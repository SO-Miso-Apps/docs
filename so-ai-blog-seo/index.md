# SO: AI Blog SEO Help Center

SO: AI Blog SEO helps merchants generate SEO-oriented SHOPLINE blog drafts, review quality, manage credits, and automate recurring content.

These docs mirror the app's in-product Help Center so merchants can browse the same onboarding guidance outside the embedded app.

## What the app covers

- Create drafts with structured inputs such as topic, keywords, article type, selected products, and merchant notes.
- Tune tone, target audience, business goal, proof points, required facts, and prohibited claims before generation.
- Review, rewrite, add cover images, and publish when the draft is ready.
- Track monthly and top-up credits, then enable automation once the workflow feels predictable.

## Recommended first workflow

1. Configure reusable defaults in Settings.
2. Generate one strong manual draft with a clear topic and primary keyword.
3. Review the draft, quality signals, and billing usage before regenerating.
4. Turn on automation only after the manual workflow feels consistent.
